# Keybinds Setup

- Set using the respective `sxhkd`/`xbindkeys` configuration file, or using GUI
  method of configuration. (Most reliable currently)

- Format: `KeyCombination`:`Action`:`Description (optional)`

1. `Alt+1` `Switch to workspace 1`
1. `Alt+2` `Switch to workspace 2`
1. `Alt+3` `Switch to workspace 3`
1. `Alt+4` `Switch to workspace 4`
1. `Alt+5` `Switch to workspace 5`
1. `Alt+6` `Switch to workspace 6`

1. `Alt+h` `Switch to left of current workspace.`
1. `Alt+j` `Switch to right of current workspace.`
1. `Alt+k` `Switch to above of current workspace.`
1. `Alt+l` `Switch to below of current workspace.`
